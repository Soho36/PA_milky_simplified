How much cushion pays, and how much protects

1,280 replays compare a daily minimum payout request against a daily maximum one, across the same reserve grid ($0–$10,000 above the frozen failure floor, in $100 steps through $3,000–$4,500 and $6,000–$7,500), two products and eight historical windows. Pipelines and funding are frozen: $5,000 plus $200 a month, 20 shared seats, a 2-spare shelf, and a pass must activate at the next daily check or be forfeited. The cash-best reserve and the reserve that keeps the book alive are not the same number.
