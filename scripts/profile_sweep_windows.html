    What the numbers say

      Net profit by entry hour

      Each bar is one window traded on its own $10,000 account for the whole tape, after commission. Hours are Tallinn wall-clock; the second label is the approximate New York hour. Click a bar to open that window below.

      Reward against risk

      Net profit against each window's worst closed-trade drawdown. The diagonals mark recovery factor 1, 2 and 4: a window on the RF 2 line earned twice its worst drawdown.

        Scorecard

        Every gauge for every window. Click a column heading to sort and a row to open the window. The curve column shows each window's own shape on its own scale.

        Net profit by calendar year

        Equity curves on one scale

        End-of-day balance for every window, all drawn against the same dollar axis so their size and shape compare directly. The hairline is the $10,000 start. Click a curve to open it.
