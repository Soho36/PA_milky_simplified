Legacy 25K book with evaluations outside the seat cap

The best ongoing-cash setting from the capacity study, replayed day by day over the RR 1.00 tape. $5,000 up front and $200 a month buy evaluations ($33/month, 3 MNQ, $1,500 target and drawdown, $125 activation). Up to 20 live PAs plus spares, a 10-spare shelf, 20 evaluations at once started in batches. Every PA asks daily for the minimum payout once its balance sits above $28,100.
