Question answered
Do the cadence findings hold under the same cash budgets and 20-live-PA limit?

What we learned
This provides the fairer bridge between the old cadence study and the purchase studies: shared
settings reproduce exactly when the assumptions match. Funding and capacity constraints can
change which withdrawal/purchase combination is attractive.

How to interpret it
There are 372 cadence settings across four funding scenarios, with separate fixed-withdrawal
purchase comparisons. Replacement accounts are immediately available when affordable;
evaluations are not modeled.
