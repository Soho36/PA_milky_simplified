Question answered
How does a $30,000 balance target affect monthly $500 withdrawals?

What we learned
This example retains a voluntary buffer and also attempts a final permitted withdrawal. It
separates cash received during trading from cash only extracted at closing. The saved run
produced $357,000.00 net cash during operation; $79,443.98 was received at closing, making
$436,443.98 combined net cash. It opened 79 PAs in total and had 17 alive before any closing
withdrawal. Net cash subtracts account/evaluation costs and excludes owner contributions.

How to interpret it
Historical 25K example with one account bought each month and no live-account cap or evaluation
stage. The saved configured rules apply; “no payout rules” does not remove drawdown failures.
