Question answered
Can holding all profit and asking once at the end extract it?

What we learned
One closing request is restricted by the configured payout rules, so retained trading profit
and cash received can differ greatly. The saved run produced $-15,800.00 net cash during
operation; $30,000.00 was received at closing, making $14,200.00 combined net cash. It opened
79 PAs in total and had 22 alive before any closing withdrawal. Net cash subtracts
account/evaluation costs and excludes owner contributions.

How to interpret it
Historical 25K example with one account bought each month and no live-account cap or evaluation
stage. The saved configured rules apply; “no payout rules” does not remove drawdown failures.
