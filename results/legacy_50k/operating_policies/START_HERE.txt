Question answered
How do 50K account mechanics and fees change withdrawal and purchasing choices?

What we learned
The 50K results depend on budget, purchase policy and reserve. The study separates ongoing cash
from closing withdrawals and checks an alternative interpretation of later payout restrictions.
Matched 25K controls reproduce the earlier model.

How to interpret it
Accounts can be purchased immediately here; no evaluation phase is modeled. The search is
coarse plus local refinement, not every possible setting. The more extensive matched reserve
comparison is in comparisons/legacy_25k_vs_50k/reserve_by_policy.
