# How this study works

Start with the selected budget. Buy accounts according to monthly, weekly or
current-month replacement policy, if owner cash and the 20-live-account cap permit.
The 50K seat costs $250; the matched 25K seat costs $200. Both use the same trade
sizes and tape. Fees and trading losses are different things: losses consume the
account's trading room, while fees consume owner cash.

For each tested reserve and check cadence, take minimum or permitted excess
withdrawals when eligible, leaving the selected reserve. Account checks begin in
the second calendar month. Falling below a reserve pauses withdrawals, not trading.
At the endpoint, score one permitted closing request releasing the voluntary reserve.
The sensitivity adds the later-payout retained-minimum condition for both products.

Stage one holds dollar headroom and operating policies fixed across products.
Stage two searches 50K reserves and check cadence with monthly purchases.
Stage three tries the selected 50K policies under the other acquisition choices.
These are different questions; the best adapted bundle is not an isolated DD effect.

Actual Legacy availability throughout the tape is counterfactual. The reports
model selected mechanics and payout interpretations, not every condition of firm
compliance. Read the source record before treating the numbers as executable payouts.
