What we learned
This is the history of the account-management research, from simple withdrawal examples to
evaluations and replacement risk. Start with comparisons/legacy_25k_vs_50k/march_failure_review
for the latest explanation of synchronized failures, and reserve_frontier for the daily-maximum
reserve curve. Daily-minimum has promising matched results but does not yet have its own
complete reserve frontier.

How to interpret it
Earlier folders use different assumptions. Some have unlimited live accounts; some assume
instantly available replacements. Later studies impose cash budgets and then model evaluations.
Compare settings within a study before comparing totals across studies.
