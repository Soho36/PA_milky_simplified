Net cash, and how much of it waits for the closing request

    Bar height is total net cash after every account cost, excluding contributions. The faded top segment is the single firm-permitted closing request, which only arrives at the end.

    Owner money required

      The pool is bought once at K = 5R seats; after that, only shortfalls and death replacements are purchased. Funding is supplied whenever needed, so this measures the requirement, not an affordability gate.

      Net cash per dollar of owner money

        Total net cash divided by all owner funding. Scaling R multiplies both sides, so this shows whether a bigger pool pays for itself at the same rate.
