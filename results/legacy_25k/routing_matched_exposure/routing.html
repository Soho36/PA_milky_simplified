Net cash, including the closing request

    The reference book copies every signal into each live account. The routed arms spread the same copies across accounts and must fill 100% of them. Net cash excludes owner contributions.

    Ongoing cash, before the closing request

      Routing disperses profit across many accounts, so each one clears the firm's payout gates more slowly. Where this is negative, the arm only finishes positive on the final permitted request.

      What matching the exposure costs

        Extra seats bought beyond the reference schedule, plus the minimum extra cash needed on top of the original contributions. The reference arm is zero on both by definition.

        Accounts bought, and how many were live at the peak

          Each reference purchase provisions five routed seats, because the tape peaks at five simultaneous positions. The reference book's 20-live cap is relaxed for the routed arms.

          Penalty against the reference

            Total net cash minus the reference's, at the same exposure. No routed arm in this study finishes ahead.
