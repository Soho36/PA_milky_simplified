Question answered
What does a voluntary $26,100 balance target do when payout restrictions are removed?

What we learned
This isolates our withdrawal restraint from firm payout gates. Drawdown failure mechanics still
apply even though payout restrictions are disabled. The saved run produced $107,200.00 net cash
during operation; $0.00 was received at closing, making $107,200.00 combined net cash. It
opened 79 PAs in total and had 6 alive before any closing withdrawal. Net cash subtracts
account/evaluation costs and excludes owner contributions.

How to interpret it
Historical 25K example with one account bought each month and no live-account cap or evaluation
stage. The saved configured rules apply; “no payout rules” does not remove drawdown failures.
