Question answered
What happens with $500 monthly requests and no voluntary reserve?

What we learned
This is the early configured-rulebook baseline. It extracts cash but leaves relatively few
survivors; later studies investigate whether retaining a reserve improves that trade-off. The
saved run produced $102,700.00 net cash during operation; $0.00 was received at closing, making
$102,700.00 combined net cash. It opened 79 PAs in total and had 7 alive before any closing
withdrawal. Net cash subtracts account/evaluation costs and excludes owner contributions.

How to interpret it
Historical 25K example with one account bought each month and no live-account cap or evaluation
stage. The saved configured rules apply; “no payout rules” does not remove drawdown failures.
