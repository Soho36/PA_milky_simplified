Question answered
How much trading profit can accumulate if nothing is withdrawn?

What we learned
This is the hold benchmark. Trading profit can build in accounts while owner cash is negative
because account purchases still cost money. Paper profit is not a withdrawal. The saved run
produced $-15,800.00 net cash during operation; $0.00 was received at closing, making
$-15,800.00 combined net cash. It opened 79 PAs in total and had 22 alive before any closing
withdrawal. Net cash subtracts account/evaluation costs and excludes owner contributions.

How to interpret it
Historical 25K example with one account bought each month and no live-account cap or evaluation
stage. The saved configured rules apply; “no payout rules” does not remove drawdown failures.
