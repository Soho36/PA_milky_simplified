Question answered
What reserve works best for each withdrawal and purchase policy when each account holds one
position?

What we learned
Across 2,936 settings run in both modes, blocking lowers total cash in 2,334 and raises it in
188; the other 414 lose the whole seed either way. Headline winners fall 11–49%. 25K with
$1,000 + $200/month drops from $749,859 to $665,421 and still buys 531 PAs. 50K with $1,000 and
no contributions falls from $530,386 to $268,478, and its winner switches to monthly purchases
with maximum weekly withdrawals.

How to interpret it
There is no evaluation delay here: seats cost $200 / $250 and are available at once, so the
high-turnover winners depend on that supply. 206 settings shared with the 25K blocked-copying
optimization reproduce exactly, and no replayed winner account ever held two trades at once.
