Is replacement capacity limiting profit when each account holds one position, and does a larger
or staggered evaluation pipeline help?

What we learned
Best tested winners fall 19–24% against the non-blocking pipeline study, and every winner now
takes the minimum withdrawal with $0–$2,400 above the floor. In the main shared-seat comparison
50K leads 25K: $518,683 against $497,570 total, and $469,400 against $453,544 ongoing; without
blocking 25K led both. Expanded pipelines lift the old aggressive instant-supply policy only to
$325,884 (25K) and $335,443 (50K), about half its instant-supply cash. The best daily-maximum
cushion is still $6,800 for both products.

How to interpret it
shared_seats reserves one of 20 places for each live PA, activated spare or in-flight
evaluation; evals_outside_cap is a separate sensitivity. Anchors and controls come from the
blocked eval_supply and reserve_by_policy studies, and all 16 controls reproduce, including
per-trade account assignments. The capacity chart is not drawn here: it needs ReportLab, which
the venv lacks.
