Question answered
Does daily minimum improve the reserve cash/survival trade-off when each account holds one
position?

What we learned
Blocking lowers total cash in 880 of the 1,280 settings run in both modes and raises it in 400.
Minimum still leads on best tested ongoing cash: $329,932 at $3,500 against $319,487 at $4,000
for 25K, and $440,140 at $3,000 against $337,153 at $1,000 for 50K. At $6,700–$6,800 both rules
keep all 20 PAs, where non-blocking maximum lost most of its book at $6,700. Blocking itself
spreads balances — the mean spread among frozen-floor PAs is two to three times larger — so
maximum is no longer synchronized. Aged-PA deaths stop from $6,400 under minimum against $6,200
under maximum for 25K, and at no tested reserve for 50K.

How to interpret it
Same grid, $5,700 probe, pipelines and windows as the non-blocking study. 416 maximum rows
reproduce the blocked frontier and 10 settings reproduce the blocked March review, including
per-trade account assignments; none of the replayed PA histories held two trades at once. The
paired chart is not drawn here: it needs ReportLab, which the venv lacks.
