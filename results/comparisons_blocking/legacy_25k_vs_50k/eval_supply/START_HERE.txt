Question answered
Can evaluations traded on the strategy supply enough replacement PAs when each account holds
one position?

What we learned
Across 4,912 settings run in both modes, blocking lowers total cash in 3,030 and raises it in
1,478; the other 404 lose the seed either way. Best tested totals fall 19–25% for every product
and budget. Evaluation supply removes the churn: 25K with $1,000 + $200/month earns $445,315
with 66 funded PAs, against $665,421 and 531 PAs with instant $200 seats. 50K now leads 25K in
three of the four budgets; 25K leads only with $1,000 and no contributions. All eight winners
keep five spares, seven take the minimum checked monthly, and a funded PA costs about
$186–$266.

How to interpret it
Evaluations trade one position at a time in both trees, so the EODMAE check is identical to the
non-blocking study. 123 settings shared with the 25K blocked-pipeline study reproduce it
exactly, including per-trade account assignments. The short-month columns are not comparable
with the saved non-blocking study: a later engine change altered how supply-limited days are
logged.
