Question answered
Which account size and withdrawal/replacement approach works best when each account holds one
position?

What we learned
So far reserve_by_policy, spare_shelf, eval_supply and pipeline_capacity have been rerun.
Blocking lowers cash in most matched settings. With instant supply the winners keep their shape
(prompt replacement, maximum withdrawals, little or no reserve) and 25K leads 50K in every
funded budget. Once supply is limited, winners switch to minimum withdrawals and buy far fewer
accounts; with evaluations traded on the tape, 50K leads 25K in three of the four budgets and
in the main pipeline search.

How to interpret it
Remaining reruns, in order: reserve_frontier, reserve_frontier_minimum, march_failure_review.
