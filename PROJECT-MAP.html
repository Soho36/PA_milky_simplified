What each file in a study folder is for

This is the distinction most worth re-reading after a break, because getting it backwards means quoting stale numbers with full confidence: the runners never overwrite anything you wrote.

A rerun replaces the generated report, the CSV and JSON, and the winner ledgers in place. A changed study design therefore needs a new output directory, or it silently overwrites evidence for a design that no longer exists.

Every one of these folders carries a RUN.md with its own regeneration command, so none of them has to be reconstructed from memory.

The __adapted_search folder is the same fixed-policy baseline plus the cushion re-optimization experiment — its baseline cash is identical on purpose. It is the folder to open for rule-effect questions, and also the one whose historical $2,200 figure is easiest to misquote as the price of the whole rulebook.

Alongside them, results/sweeps/ holds four cushion grids: RR__monthly_500 coarse and fine, RR__monthly_maximum, and one on the GG tape. They are historical searches with no terminal withdrawal, and they do not embed a full config or input hashes — their provenance cannot be verified from the saved files alone.

Before you put two numbers side by side

  • Uncapped dollars are not funded dollarsThe amount and cadence studies had no live-account cap and never ran out of cash. Their totals explain mechanisms; ranking them against the funded studies compares different worlds.
  • Comparable within one budgetThe three funded studies are internally comparable inside each of the four funding scenarios — not across them, and not against the uncapped generation.
  • retain_N is a threshold, not a floorA $31,900 retained balance is what you request down to. Trading losses can still take an account below it, and a faster eligibility check is not a guaranteed payout.
  • Terminal cash is one requestTerminal-inclusive totals add a single firm-permitted closing request — never an unrestricted liquidation, except in the folder explicitly named terminal_idealized.
  • These are dataset totalsEvery figure is a full-dataset total across Jan 2020 to Jul 2026, not annual income. An endpoint the dataset happens to end on is not a forecast.
  • Rule-removal deltas do not add upFixed-policy removal and cushion-adapted removal answer different questions. Individual deltas are not additive components of one total cost.
  • Accounts are not independent trialsOne tape drives every cohort in every study, so more accounts is not more evidence. Start dates and calendar phases stay untested except where PHASE_SENSITIVITY.md records supplied checks.
  • The audit checks arithmetic, not truthCONSISTENCY_AUDIT.md verifies saved identities, CSV/JSON agreement, input hashes and shared controls. It is not out-of-sample validation, and it certifies no reader prose.
